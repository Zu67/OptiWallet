# OptiWallet

Credit card optimization tracker. Cards you pick from the catalog are the wallet, and the app stores that choice in this browser.

The catalog has 69 cards: Chase 18, Amex 14, Capital One 8, Citi 6, Bank of America 6, Barclays 6, Discover 4, US Bank 3, Wells Fargo 3, and Bilt 1. The issuer label is "US Bank", with no periods. A card record has an annual fee, category multipliers, a cents-per-point valuation, credits, perks, and transfer partners.

- **Overview.** The three figures are labeled "Total Annual Fees", "Wallet Size", and "Avg Point Value". The fee total is a `$` and the sum, with no thousands separator. The line under it says "Across {n} active cards" and counts cards whose fee is 0. Wallet Size has the line "Active Accounts". The average is shown with two decimal places and a `¢` sign. A bar chart titled "Annual Fee Breakdown" lists cards with an annual fee, highest first. A bar's label keeps the text before the first ®, then removes the first "Credit Card". That makes separate cards share a label: both Southwest Rapid Rewards cards become "Southwest Rapid Rewards", the Gold Card and the Green Card become "American Express", the three Delta SkyMiles cards become "Delta SkyMiles", both Citi AAdvantage cards become "Citi", and both BoA Premium Rewards cards become "BoA Premium Rewards". World of Hyatt Credit Card becomes "World of Hyatt". A card with no annual fee is left off that chart, so the four Discover it cards and Wyndham Rewards Earner® never appear. If every selected card has no annual fee, the chart says "No annual fees to display". A pie chart titled "Wallet Composition" counts cards by issuer.
- **Strategy.** The heading is "I am spending on...". The picker opens on Dining. The percent on a card is its multiplier times its cents-per-point number, shown with two decimal places and a `%` sign. The earn line is that multiplier, `×`, and "pts". The category rate is the first matching multiplier on that card, not the highest. No card in this catalog has both `hotel` and `hotel_hyatt`. World of Hyatt's Hyatt rate is its `hotel_hyatt` entry at 4. The winner badge says "Best Choice" even when the percent is negative. The next three cards are under "Alternative Options". With one selected card that heading is still shown and no rows follow it. Dining uses a dining rate. Groceries uses `grocery` or `grocery_online`. Flights uses `travel_air`, or another travel rate whose name does not contain "portal". On Chase Sapphire Reserve that skips the 5x portal-flight rate and uses the later `travel_general` rate of 3, so that card's row is 3× and 6.15%. General Travel uses any rate whose name contains "travel", including portal rates. On that same card the first match is `travel_portal_hotel` at 10, so that card's row is 10× and 20.50%. Gas uses `gas` or `rotating`. Amazon uses `amazon` or `grocery`. Hyatt uses a non-portal `hotel_hyatt`, `hotel`, or `travel_general` rate. Marriott uses a non-portal `hotel_marriott`, `hotel`, or `travel_general` rate. Rent uses a `rent` rate, and otherwise subtracts 3 from that percent. Reserve has no rent rate, so a 1x base and a 2.05 cent value become -0.95%, the note says "Incurs ~3% Transaction Fee", and the warning says "Warning: -3% fee applied". Online Groceries, the portal categories, Transit, Hilton, IHG, Hotels, and every remaining category use only the card's base rate.
- **My Wallet.** The heading is "My Wallet", and the line under it says "You have {n} cards. Here is your benefits breakdown." Each selected card is drawn with the issuer, the card name, the fee as `${fee}/yr` or "NO FEE", and the same placeholder number, •••• •••• •••• 1234. A card with at least one credit shows those credits under "Annual Value & Credits". A card with an empty credit list omits that block. The rates are under "Earning Power": the base rate is labeled "Base", then the first five category rates. A category label replaces only the first underscore, so `travel_portal_hotel` is shown as "travel portal_hotel". The perks are under "Key Protections & Perks".
- **Points.** Each card has its own balance box, stored in this browser. An empty or non-numeric entry is saved as 0. Cards are grouped by the exact `rewardName`. "Cash Back" is one group of 12 cards across Chase, Amex, Capital One, Bank of America, Discover, and US Bank. "Marriott Bonvoy Points" puts the Chase card and the Amex card in one group. "ThankYou Points" and "ThankYou Points (via Cash)" stay separate. The group total adds the balances. The dollar estimate adds each balance times that card's cents-per-point value, divided by 100. The `¢ / pt` badge and the transfer-partner list come from the first selected card of that name in catalog order. Later cards do not add partners. That list differs inside Ultimate Rewards, Membership Rewards, ThankYou Points, and Capital One Miles. Sapphire Reserve, Sapphire Preferred, Ink Business Preferred, the Platinum Card, the American Express Gold Card, Strata Premier, Venture X, Venture Rewards, and VentureOne name partners. Freedom Unlimited, Freedom Flex, Ink Business Cash, Ink Business Unlimited, the Green Card, Business Platinum, Business Gold, Blue Business Plus, Custom Cash, Rewards+, Spark Miles for Business, and Venture X Business do not. The header says the examples are 2.05¢ for Chase and Bilt and 2.0¢ for Amex. Cards that share a reward name in the catalog also share one cents-per-point value.
- **Credits.** A checklist of the selected cards' statement credits. Only the first `$` figure in the credit text is counted, and the digits stop before a letter, so "United Club Membership ($650 value)" counts as 650. "$200 Hilton Credit ($50/qtr)" counts as 200. "$125 Flight Discount (after $20k spend)" counts as 125, not 20 and not 20000. "$200 Bonus after $200k spend" counts as 200. A credit with no `$` figure, such as "Two 5k Mile Rebates", counts as 0. The screen adds those amounts and the amounts whose boxes are checked. With no credits, it says "No Credits Available" and names Sapphire Reserve and Amex Platinum.
- **Add Cards.** The catalog. The first tab state is Overview. An effect runs when the saved card count changes. If that count is 0 and the tab is not the catalog, it switches here. The effect does not depend on the tab, so choosing Overview, Strategy, My Wallet, Points, or Credits, or clicking the OptiWallet logo, while the count stays 0, leaves "Your wallet is empty" on screen. That screen also says "Start by adding cards from the catalog to unlock your personalized financial dashboard." The button says "Browse Catalog". Issuer sections start closed. The filter starts on Personal. A business card is one whose lowercased name contains "business", "ink", "spark", "corporate", or "biz". The issuer is lowercased beside that test and is not used. In this catalog, "corporate" and "biz" match no card. The ten matches are Ink Business Preferred®, Ink Business Cash®, Ink Business Unlimited®, Business Platinum Card®, Business Gold Card®, Blue Business® Plus, Alaska Airlines Business, Spark Miles for Business, Capital One Venture X Business, and Spark Cash Plus. Spark Cash Plus matches only because the name contains "spark". Search matches the card name or the issuer. The placeholder is "Search by card name or bank...". No match says "No cards found" and "Try adjusting your search or filters."

A missing `theme` key follows the system preference, and the first load writes that choice as `dark` or `light`. After the key exists, the system preference is not read again. The other keys are `optiWallet_cards`, `optiWallet_balances`, and `optiWallet_creditUsage`. A saved value that is not JSON throws while the page is loading.

The screen code is in `src/`. `src/constants.ts` is the card catalog. `src/components/` holds Overview, Strategy, My Wallet, Points, Credits, and the catalog screen.

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```
