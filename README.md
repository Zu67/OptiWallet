# OptiWallet

Credit card optimization tracker. Cards you pick from the catalog are the wallet, and the app stores that choice in this browser.

The catalog has 69 cards: Chase 18, Amex 14, Capital One 8, Citi 6, Bank of America 6, Barclays 6, Discover 4, US Bank 3, Wells Fargo 3, and Bilt 1. The issuer label is "US Bank", with no periods. A card record has an annual fee, category multipliers, a cents-per-point valuation, credits, perks, and transfer partners.

- **Overview.** The three figures are labeled "Total Annual Fees", "Wallet Size", and "Avg Point Value". The fee total is a `$` and the sum, with no thousands separator. The line under it says "Across {n} active cards" and counts cards whose fee is 0. Wallet Size has the line "Active Accounts". The average is shown with two decimal places and a `¢` sign. A bar chart titled "Annual Fee Breakdown" lists cards with an annual fee, highest first. A bar's label keeps the text before the first ®, then removes the first "Credit Card". That makes separate cards share a label: both Southwest Rapid Rewards cards become "Southwest Rapid Rewards", the Gold Card and the Green Card become "American Express", the three Delta SkyMiles cards become "Delta SkyMiles", both Citi AAdvantage cards become "Citi", and both BoA Premium Rewards cards become "BoA Premium Rewards". World of Hyatt Credit Card becomes "World of Hyatt". A card with no annual fee is left off that chart, so the four Discover it cards and Wyndham Rewards Earner® never appear. Wyndham Rewards Earner® Plus costs 75 and its bar is labeled "Wyndham Rewards Earner", the same words as the zero-fee card. The fee axis is hidden, so the dollar amount is only in the tooltip, labeled "Annual Fee". Equal fees keep the earlier catalog card. The pie slices follow catalog order. Its six colors then repeat, so a seventh issuer uses the same blue as the first. If every selected card has no annual fee, the chart says "No annual fees to display". A pie chart titled "Wallet Composition" counts cards by issuer.
- **Strategy.** The heading is "I am spending on...". The picker opens on Dining. The percent on a card is its multiplier times its cents-per-point number, shown with two decimal places and a `%` sign. The earn line is that multiplier, `×`, and "pts". The category rate is the first matching multiplier on that card, not the highest. No card in this catalog has both `hotel` and `hotel_hyatt`. World of Hyatt's Hyatt rate is its `hotel_hyatt` entry at 4. The winner badge says "Best Choice" even when the percent is negative, and that percent stays green at 0 or below. An alternative at 0% or below is red. Equal percents keep the earlier card in the catalog. Every multiplier in the catalog has a description, so a matched category shows that description. The fallbacks "Category Bonus", "Direct Booking", and "Waives fees" are not used. An unmatched category shows "Base rate", except rent. With no cards this screen is not mounted, so "Add cards to see strategy" is not shown. The next three cards are under "Alternative Options". With one selected card that heading is still shown and no rows follow it. Dining uses a dining rate. Groceries uses `grocery` or `grocery_online`. Flights uses `travel_air`, or another travel rate whose name does not contain "portal". On Chase Sapphire Reserve that skips the 5x portal-flight rate and uses the later `travel_general` rate of 3, so that card's row is 3× and 6.15%. General Travel uses any rate whose name contains "travel", including portal rates. On that same card the first match is `travel_portal_hotel` at 10, so that card's row is 10× and 20.50%. Gas uses `gas` or `rotating`. Amazon uses `amazon` or `grocery`. Hyatt uses a non-portal `hotel_hyatt`, `hotel`, or `travel_general` rate. Marriott uses a non-portal `hotel_marriott`, `hotel`, or `travel_general` rate. Rent uses a `rent` rate, and otherwise subtracts 3 from that percent. Reserve has no rent rate, so a 1x base and a 2.05 cent value become -0.95%, the note says "Incurs ~3% Transaction Fee", and the warning says "Warning: -3% fee applied". Online Groceries, the portal categories, Transit, Hilton, IHG, Hotels, and every remaining category use only the card's base rate.
- **My Wallet.** The heading is "My Wallet", and the line under it says "You have {n} cards. Here is your benefits breakdown." Each selected card is drawn with the issuer, the card name, the fee as `${fee}/yr` or "NO FEE", and the same placeholder number, •••• •••• •••• 1234. A card with at least one credit shows those credits under "Annual Value & Credits". A card with an empty credit list omits that block. The rates are under "Earning Power": the base rate is labeled "Base", then the first five category rates. A category label replaces only the first underscore, so `travel_portal_hotel` is shown as "travel portal_hotel". The perks are under "Key Protections & Perks".
- **Points.** The heading is "Total Points Value". Each card has its own balance box, labeled "Enter balance", stored in this browser. An empty or non-numeric entry is saved as 0, and a decimal is cut off at the dot, so `10.9` is saved as 10. A negative number is kept. A stored 0 is shown as an empty box. Cards are grouped by the exact `rewardName`. "Cash Back" is one group of 12 cards across Chase, Amex, Capital One, Bank of America, Discover, and US Bank. "Marriott Bonvoy Points" puts the Chase card and the Amex card in one group. "ThankYou Points" and "ThankYou Points (via Cash)" stay separate. The group total adds the balances and is shown with thousands separators, so 1000 is "1,000", followed by "pts". The dollar estimate adds each balance times that card's cents-per-point value, divided by 100, and is always shown with two decimal places as "≈ $6.15 value". The badge is "~2.05¢ / pt", and that valuation and the transfer-partner list come from the first selected card of that name in catalog order. Later cards do not add partners. The "Transfer Partners" block is omitted when that list is empty. That list differs inside Ultimate Rewards, Membership Rewards, ThankYou Points, and Capital One Miles. Sapphire Reserve, Sapphire Preferred, Ink Business Preferred, the Platinum Card, the American Express Gold Card, Strata Premier, Venture X, Venture Rewards, and VentureOne name partners. Freedom Unlimited, Freedom Flex, Ink Business Cash, Ink Business Unlimited, the Green Card, Business Platinum, Business Gold, Blue Business Plus, Custom Cash, Rewards+, Spark Miles for Business, and Venture X Business do not. The header says the examples are 2.05¢ for Chase and Bilt and 2.0¢ for Amex. Cards that share a reward name in the catalog also share one cents-per-point value.
- **Credits.** A checklist of the selected cards' statement credits. Only the first `$` figure in the credit text is counted, and the digits stop before a decimal point or a letter, so "United Club Membership ($650 value)" counts as 650. "$200 Hilton Credit ($50/qtr)" counts as 200. "$125 Flight Discount (after $20k spend)" counts as 125, not 20 and not 20000. "$200 Bonus after $200k spend" counts as 200. A credit with no `$` figure, such as "Two 5k Mile Rebates", "CLEAR Plus Credit", or "Free Night Award (35k)", counts as 0 and the row does not show a dollar amount. The heading "Total Value Extracted" shows the checked dollar sum, a slash, and the dollar sum of every credit. "Credits Used" and "Credits Remaining" count credit lines, not dollars. The percent line is that dollar ratio rounded to the nearest whole number, then "% Realized". When every parsed amount is 0, the percent is 0. A checked credit is struck through. "No Credits Available" appears only when no selected card has a credit list. Its text says "None of your active cards have annual credits associated with them. Add premium cards like Sapphire Reserve or Amex Platinum to track credits." A card whose credits all parse to 0 still shows the checklist.
- **Add Cards.** The catalog. The first tab state is Overview. An effect runs when the saved card count changes. If that count is 0 and the tab is not the catalog, it switches here. The effect does not depend on the tab, so choosing Overview, Strategy, My Wallet, Points, or Credits, or clicking the OptiWallet logo, while the count stays 0, leaves "Your wallet is empty" on screen. That screen also says "Start by adding cards from the catalog to unlock your personalized financial dashboard." The button says "Browse Catalog". Issuer sections start closed. The heading is "Card Catalog", and the line under it says "Select cards to add to your wallet." The filter starts on Personal, and the buttons are Personal, Business, and All. Personal hides the 10 business cards, so the first view has 59 cards. A business card is one whose lowercased name contains "business", "ink", "spark", "corporate", or "biz". The issuer is lowercased beside that test and is not used. In this catalog, "corporate" and "biz" match no card. The ten matches are Ink Business Preferred®, Ink Business Cash®, Ink Business Unlimited®, Business Platinum Card®, Business Gold Card®, Blue Business® Plus, Alaska Airlines Business, Spark Miles for Business, Capital One Venture X Business, and Spark Cash Plus. Spark Cash Plus matches only because the name contains "spark". Search matches a lowercased substring of the card name or the issuer. The placeholder is "Search by card name or bank...". Searching "Ink" while Personal is selected finds nothing, because those cards are hidden by the filter. No match says "No cards found" and "Try adjusting your search or filters." Sections follow the issuer enum: Chase, Amex, Capital One, Citi, Discover, Wells Fargo, US Bank, Bank of America, Barclays, then Bilt. `Other` is in the enum and has no cards. A closed section says "{n} cards available", and "{n} added" when any of its visible cards are selected. The closed badge says "{n} Added". A selected card shows a check, and clicking it again removes the card. The fee line says "Free" when the fee is 0, and otherwise `$` plus the fee, with no "/yr". Under that, the first two category rates are shown, and every underscore is replaced, so `travel_portal_hotel` is "travel portal hotel". Eight cards have no category rates and omit those chips: Ink Business Unlimited®, Blue Business® Plus, Citi Double Cash®, BoA Unlimited Cash Rewards®, Spark Cash Plus, U.S. Bank Smartly™, Wells Fargo Active Cash®, and Discover it® Miles.

The header labels are Overview, Strategy, My Wallet, Points, Credits, and Add Cards. The same labels are in the mobile menu. The theme control has no text: dark mode shows a sun icon and light mode shows a moon icon. A missing `theme` key follows the system preference, and the first load writes that choice as `dark` or `light`. Any saved value other than the exact string `dark` is treated as light and rewritten to `light`. The other keys are `optiWallet_cards`, `optiWallet_balances`, and `optiWallet_creditUsage`. A saved value that is not JSON throws while the page is loading. A cards value that parses to something other than an array throws when the list is filtered. Removing a card leaves its balance and its checked credits in those two keys. Adding the same card again restores both. With no saved cards, the first paint is the Overview tab, so "Your wallet is empty" appears, and the effect then opens Add Cards.

The screen code is in `src/`. `src/constants.ts` is the card catalog. `src/components/` holds Overview, Strategy, My Wallet, Points, Credits, and the catalog screen.

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```
